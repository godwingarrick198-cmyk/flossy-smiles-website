Our Services

Cosmetic Treatments We Offer

A cosmetic dentist focuses on treatments that enhance the appearance of your smile. Our team offers a comprehensive suite of services for patients of all ages.

Porcelain Veneers

Thin shells that cover the front of teeth to improve shape, color, and symmetry.

Teeth Whitening

Professional whitening treatments to brighten your smile. Note: whitening is not a permanent treatment and may need maintenance over time.

Invisalign / Clear Aligners

Clear, custom plastic aligners that align teeth gradually over a period of months to years.

Dental Bonding

Tooth-colored resin applied to repair chips, gaps, or discoloration.

Inlays & Onlays

Custom restorations that repair damaged teeth while preserving as much natural structure as possible.

Enamel & Gum Contouring

Reshaping treatments that refine the balance between your teeth and gum line.

Smile Makeovers

A Plan Built Around Your Smile

A smile makeover is a customized combination of treatments — such as veneers, whitening, and bonding — designed to fit your unique mouth and goals. Smile makeovers are common before weddings or major life events, but there's no need to wait for a special occasion.

The procedures included in a smile makeover require regular oral hygiene care and may need maintenance over time. For example, teeth whitening is not a permanent treatment.

Cosmetic FAQ

Common Questions About Cosmetic Dentistry

What is a cosmetic dentist?

A cosmetic dentist is a dental specialist who focuses primarily on cosmetic and aesthetic treatments for the purpose of enhancing patients' smiles, including bonding, whitening, veneers, and more.

Is teeth whitening permanent?

No. Teeth whitening is not a permanent treatment and typically needs maintenance over time to preserve results.

How long does Invisalign treatment take?

Invisalign uses clear, custom aligners created to align teeth over the span of a few months to a few years, depending on your individual case.

What is included in a smile makeover?

A smile makeover is customized per patient and may combine treatments such as veneers, whitening, and bonding. Your specific plan will be discussed during your consultation.

Do I need a consultation first?

Yes. Every cosmetic treatment plan begins with a free consultation so our team can understand your goals and recommend the right combination of services.
