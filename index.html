Dental Implants

A Long-Term Solution for Missing Teeth

Dental implants are small, screw-shaped titanium posts that function similarly to the root of a natural tooth. Once placed in the jawbone, the post fuses with the bone to create a solid, stable foundation for a replacement tooth.

  • Comprehensive evaluation using x-rays and imaging to plan your treatment
  • Bone grafting available first, if the jawbone needs additional support
  • Most implant placements are completed under local anesthesia
  • All-on-4 and All-on-X options for full-arch restoration
